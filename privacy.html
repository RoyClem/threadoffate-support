Privacy Policy

Thread of Fate — effective 3 October 2026

Summary

Thread of Fate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests of its own.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your iPhone, using Apple's standard app storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer:

  • A display name you optionally enter for the local high-score list
  • Your local top-ten scores
  • Lifetime play statistics used to award achievements
  • Which achievements you have unlocked
  • Your sound on/off preference

Deleting the app permanently removes all of it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app submits your score to a Game Center leaderboard so it can be ranked against other players. This is handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive your Apple Account, your Game Center identity, or any other personal information through it.

Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. You can manage or disable Game Center in the Settings app on your device.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is collected or transmitted
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs or services
  • No account creation or sign-in
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location
  • No in-app purchases
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
